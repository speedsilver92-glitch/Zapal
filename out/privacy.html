Privacy policy

This notice explains how information is handled when you use the ZAPAL SK website and contact form.

Who is responsible?

Zapal SK s. r. o., IČO 53989775, at Stará Vajnorská 1367/4, 831 04 Bratislava - mestská časť Nové Mesto, Slovakia, is the business identified on this website. For privacy-related requests, use the contact form or write to this address.

Information you provide

The enquiry form requests your name or company name, email address, mobile contact, area of interest, and message. Your chosen contact channel, consent, submission time, and a request reference are also recorded. A hashed network identifier is used to limit automated or excessive submissions.

Why we use it

Your information is used to record your enquiry, evaluate your requirements, and support a response. It is not submitted to marketing lists. Please do not include sensitive personal information, passwords, payment details, or confidential documents in your message.

Email and WhatsApp

Enquiries are stored in the website database. When email delivery is configured, an email notification containing your enquiry is sent to the company. Where email-client handoff is used, you must complete sending in your email application.

Choosing WhatsApp prepares an external message containing the details you supplied. You must review and send the message in WhatsApp. When a direct recipient has not yet been configured, you will need to select the intended contact yourself. WhatsApp’s own privacy terms apply to its service.

Maps and external services

The Contacts page embeds Google Maps to show the published business address. Loading the map connects your browser to Google, which may receive technical data such as your IP address and apply its own cookies and privacy terms. External links to Google Maps and WhatsApp open their respective services. Website fonts and photographs are served locally; no advertising analytics are installed by this application.

Access, retention, and your choices

Contact submissions are not publicly accessible. Authorized operators can access them to manage enquiries and should retain them only for as long as necessary for that purpose and any applicable legal obligations.

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, or withdraw consent for future contact, using the contact form or the postal address above. Include your enquiry reference when available. You may also contact the relevant data protection authority regarding your rights.
